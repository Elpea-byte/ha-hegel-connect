# Blueprints

Ready-made automations with a one-click import (Settings > Automations & scenes > Blueprints). They only list this integration's own entities.

- **Smart plug: reconnect at once**: Asks Hegel Connect to reconnect every few seconds after the plug that powers the amplifier turns on, until the amplifier answers (no waiting for the next retry).
  [![Open your Home Assistant instance and show the blueprint import dialog.](https://my.home-assistant.io/badges/blueprint_import.svg)](https://my.home-assistant.io/redirect/blueprint_import/?blueprint_url=https%3A%2F%2Fraw.githubusercontent.com%2FElpea-byte%2Fha-hegel-connect%2Fmain%2Fblueprints%2Fautomation%2Fhegel_connect%2Fsmart_plug_reconnect.yaml)
- **Radio at a set time**: Switches the amplifier on at a set time on the days you choose and starts one of your radio favorites, at a volume you pick (never above the maximum volume).
  [![Open your Home Assistant instance and show the blueprint import dialog.](https://my.home-assistant.io/badges/blueprint_import.svg)](https://my.home-assistant.io/redirect/blueprint_import/?blueprint_url=https%3A%2F%2Fraw.githubusercontent.com%2FElpea-byte%2Fha-hegel-connect%2Fmain%2Fblueprints%2Fautomation%2Fhegel_connect%2Fradio_at_time.yaml)
- **Standby when idle or nobody is home**: Puts the amplifier in standby after it has been idle on the network input for a while, and optionally when everyone has left home; other inputs are never switched off.
  [![Open your Home Assistant instance and show the blueprint import dialog.](https://my.home-assistant.io/badges/blueprint_import.svg)](https://my.home-assistant.io/redirect/blueprint_import/?blueprint_url=https%3A%2F%2Fraw.githubusercontent.com%2FElpea-byte%2Fha-hegel-connect%2Fmain%2Fblueprints%2Fautomation%2Fhegel_connect%2Fstandby_when_idle.yaml)
- **Lower the maximum volume at night**: Sets the maximum volume lower at night and back in the morning; a volume above the new maximum comes down at once.
  [![Open your Home Assistant instance and show the blueprint import dialog.](https://my.home-assistant.io/badges/blueprint_import.svg)](https://my.home-assistant.io/redirect/blueprint_import/?blueprint_url=https%3A%2F%2Fraw.githubusercontent.com%2FElpea-byte%2Fha-hegel-connect%2Fmain%2Fblueprints%2Fautomation%2Fhegel_connect%2Fnight_volume_limit.yaml)
- **Log or announce what is playing** (new in 0.7): Writes every new track of the now-playing event to the logbook and, if you choose an action, runs it too (for example a notification); nothing is sent unless you choose one. Optional filters on audio quality and on internet radio. Enable the now-playing event first: it is off by default.
  [![Open your Home Assistant instance and show the blueprint import dialog.](https://my.home-assistant.io/badges/blueprint_import.svg)](https://my.home-assistant.io/redirect/blueprint_import/?blueprint_url=https%3A%2F%2Fraw.githubusercontent.com%2FElpea-byte%2Fha-hegel-connect%2Fmain%2Fblueprints%2Fautomation%2Fhegel_connect%2Fnow_playing.yaml)

- **Follow the TV** (new in 0.8, not released yet): When the TV switches on, the amplifier switches on and goes to the TV's input (optionally at a volume); when the TV switches off it goes to standby, but only if it is still on that input, so music you started in the meantime keeps playing. Works with a TV media player, a switch, a remote or a power-measuring plug.
  [![Open your Home Assistant instance and show the blueprint import dialog.](https://my.home-assistant.io/badges/blueprint_import.svg)](https://my.home-assistant.io/redirect/blueprint_import/?blueprint_url=https%3A%2F%2Fraw.githubusercontent.com%2FElpea-byte%2Fha-hegel-connect%2Fmain%2Fblueprints%2Fautomation%2Fhegel_connect%2Ffollow_the_tv.yaml)
- **Switch on when the smart plug turns on** (new in 0.8, not released yet): Waits until the amplifier answers after the plug turns on (asking Hegel Connect to reconnect meanwhile), switches it on from standby and optionally goes to an input and a volume.
  [![Open your Home Assistant instance and show the blueprint import dialog.](https://my.home-assistant.io/badges/blueprint_import.svg)](https://my.home-assistant.io/redirect/blueprint_import/?blueprint_url=https%3A%2F%2Fraw.githubusercontent.com%2FElpea-byte%2Fha-hegel-connect%2Fmain%2Fblueprints%2Fautomation%2Fhegel_connect%2Fswitch_on_from_the_mains.yaml)

## Scripts

Ready-made scripts, imported the same way (they appear under Settings > Automations & scenes > Scripts). Run them from a dashboard button, a voice command or an automation.

- **Sleep timer** (new in 0.8, not released yet): Turns the volume down in steps over the time you choose (30 minutes by default, or `minutes` for one run), then puts the amplifier in standby and sets the volume back for next time. It stops when you touch the volume or switch the amplifier off yourself; on an input with fixed volume it only waits.
  [![Open your Home Assistant instance and show the blueprint import dialog.](https://my.home-assistant.io/badges/blueprint_import.svg)](https://my.home-assistant.io/redirect/blueprint_import/?blueprint_url=https%3A%2F%2Fraw.githubusercontent.com%2FElpea-byte%2Fha-hegel-connect%2Fmain%2Fblueprints%2Fscript%2Fhegel_connect%2Fsleep_timer.yaml)
- **Announcement, then back to what played** (new in 0.8, not released yet): The [announcement recipe](announcements.md) as a script: it remembers the amplifier, speaks `message` through the amplifier's Cast entity and your text-to-speech, waits until the message has played (no fixed pause) and puts power, input, volume and mute back; then it starts again what played before (the radio does not come back by itself after a Cast message). An optional volume and language for the message. The Cast entity is often disabled: enable it under the amplifier's device and restart Home Assistant.
  [![Open your Home Assistant instance and show the blueprint import dialog.](https://my.home-assistant.io/badges/blueprint_import.svg)](https://my.home-assistant.io/redirect/blueprint_import/?blueprint_url=https%3A%2F%2Fraw.githubusercontent.com%2FElpea-byte%2Fha-hegel-connect%2Fmain%2Fblueprints%2Fscript%2Fhegel_connect%2Fannouncement.yaml)

What was tried: the four blueprints above are checked as valid by the tests, and *Follow the TV*, the sleep timer and the announcement also run in a test Home Assistant. On an H150: the sleep timer faded from 12 to 5 in two minutes, went to standby and set the volume back to 12 in standby; switching on with the plug took 30 seconds from plug on to playing input and volume; the announcement was heard and volume and input came back, and after a fix it also starts the radio again (that last part only in the tests). *Follow the TV* was not tried on a real amplifier.

The blueprints use the amplifier's own name for the network input (*Network*). Hegel Connect accepts that name even when you gave the input your own name in the options.
